# Seenty

Monitor. Detect. Respond. One platform for uptime, security and observability.

Seenty watches what you run on the internet: uptime and TLS certificates, your attack surface (domains, subdomains, cloud accounts and repositories) and the telemetry of your servers and applications, and turns what it finds into incidents your on-call team can act on.

## Open source

- **[guarda](https://github.com/SeentyHQ/guarda)**: a free CLI that checks your lockfiles and GitHub workflows against a signed feed of curated supply-chain advisories (poisoned releases, hijacked tags, malicious packages). It runs on your machine and nothing leaves it: `npx @seenty/guarda`. Browse the advisories at [guarda.seenty.com](https://guarda.seenty.com).
